Travel to Lumiere

There are several travel options:


1. Train: Eurostar to Lille. Change to SNCF to Avignon.

Booking via Trainline usually costs around £250 return (booking starts 6 months in advance)

We could get a group return booking from St. Pancras to Lille. It works out cheaper.

A group booking for 10 or more gives us a 10% discount on SNCF. (this can been arranged after signing up with our course leader David.



As usual we would have a coach from Avignon to Lumieres included in the course fee.





2. Plane from London to Marseille (there are several airlines which go to Marseille)

There is a special bus from Marseille airport to Vitrolles station to get a train to Avignon Centre. Make sure to buy your ticket before boarding. It can be done online. 

There is a bus from just outside the station to Lumières for a couple of Euros.

The train cost at least €18

A taxi from the airport to Lumières cost around €180.









3. Make your own way by car
