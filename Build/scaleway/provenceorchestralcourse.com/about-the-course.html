About the Course

The orchhestral course for adults has been running for over 15 years.

All orchestral instruments welcome. You should be about grade 5 or above standard.

Rehearsals take place in the beautiful church hall of Notre-Dame de Lumiere. Full rehearsals are condcuted by David Evans.

Often we break in to wind and string sectionals:

String sectionals will be lead by Marina Solarek, wind sectionals will be lead by David Evans.


Morning rehearsal 9.30 -12.30pm (break at 10.45)

(free lunch time: visiting the nearby picturesque villages, such as Roussillon, Lacoste, Menerbes and Gordes or just visit the local retaurant or sit at the pool with your own picnic)


Afternoon  rehearsal 5 -7 pm

(evening meal is served in the courtyard of the hotel).


One day (usually Saturday) is free. A optional bus tour to the amazing marked in Apt will be orgnaised.


The course will finish witth a concert on the last  day (Sunday) in the Church of Lumiere.

Provence Orchestral Course

The Summer course was always the desire of my co-founder of the Learning Orchestra, Anthony Greville-Bell who had family connections with Goult. The year after his death we managed to get it started for 11 Summers until Covid. We restarted it last year.

The participants are mainly from the Learning Orchestra in London but there are people from other parts of the U.K. and occasionally from other European countries.

The repertoire is of moderate difficulty from the classical or light music repertoire adapted to the instrumental forces at our disposal.

The string coach and leader is Marina Solarek who has great experience with the major symphony and opera orchestras and many years teaching experience.

David Evans
