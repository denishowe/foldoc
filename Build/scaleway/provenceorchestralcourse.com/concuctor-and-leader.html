David Evans first instrument was the recorder and violin. He started the fute at 15.

Later he studied flutewith Peter Lloyd while simultaneously studying Classics at Liverpool University.

He won a scholarship to study at the Royal Manchester College of Music with Geoffrey Gilbert during which time he played frequently with the Liverpool Philharmonic, BBC Northern.

He joined the Halle orchestra as second flute and after that joined the IOceland Symphony Orchehsra on first Flute.

After some time back in London he went as principal flute to the São Paulo Philharmonic then as co-principal to the Brazilian Symphony Orchestra in Rio.


While in Rio he conducted musicals and operettas for the British and American theatre groups as well as playing and conducting avarious of contemporary music ensembles.


After nearly 20 years in Brazil he returned to London free-lancing and teaching. David now lives in Portugal 

Marina Solarek studied violin, piano and conducting in Mannheim and Hamburg and completed her violin studies in London with Prof Yfrah Neamen. She has performed regularly in Britain and abroad with her Piano Trio, the Feinstein Ensemble and the Baroque Ensemble Kontraste. Her Piano Trio has been specialising in the performance of works by neglected women composers of the 18th, 19th and 20th Century. They have recorded the early chamber Music of Henriette Bosmans on the Toccata label in 2023. He has given the first british performance of Henriette Bosmans violin concerto with the Richmond Orchhestra in 2024. https://youtu.be/u7eCjDFPrZM?si=HzqsafQHA9h2CD80


Marina has given concerts at the South Bank and has made several broadcasts for Classic FM and BBC Radio 3. She has recently given the first performance of the Violin concerto by Henriette Bosmans with the Richmond Orchestra.


She was a member of the BBC Radio Orchestra in is now leading a busy freelance career, working with the Orchestra of the Royal Opera House Covent Garden, BBC Symphony Orchestra, West End Shows and for Film and Television.


Marina is Artistic Director of the Youth Music Centre in North West London. She is the co-founder and tutor for the highly successful “Arpeggione” residential viola course which she runs together with Jacky Woods. She has taught at the Royal College Junior Department, has coached on many residential chamber courses, conducts amateur orchestras and has given Masterclasses at the University of Belfast. In her free time she takes ballet classes with the Roger Tully Collective.
