Provence Orchestral Course

The Hotellerie Notre Dame de Lumieres is 38km east of Avignon on the main road to Apt. and is a converted convent with simple, but comfortable accommodation, good food and a swimming pool in a peaceful wooded location. Rehearsals are in the mornings and early evening, leaving the afternoon free for visiting the nearby picturesque villages, such as Roussillon, Lacoste, Menerbes and Gordes.

We will give a concert for the local residents in the village church in Goult on Sunday August 31 to mark the end of the course.

The recommended standard for the course is a good Grade 5 and above
